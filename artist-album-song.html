Worldwide pure-sales estimates include studio albums, compilations, live albums, and box sets, based on ChartMasters’ album-sales data where available. Supplemental figures use published artist, label, and industry reports; a few report broader record sales or partial totals, as noted below.

Sources for supplemental estimates
  • Alice in Chains: more than 30 million albums worldwide.
  • Audioslave: more than 8 million records worldwide; source does not separate album units.
  • Barbara Mandrell: 1 million documented U.S. album sales; no complete worldwide total found.
  • Imagine Dragons: 46 million albums and 55 million songs sold globally (2021 label release).
  • Journey: more than 100 million albums globally (band bio).
  • Maroon 5: 23.6 million album sales in the BestSellingAlbums database.
  • Run-D.M.C.: more than 20 million albums sold worldwide.
  • Soundgarden: A&M catalog passed 20 million albums by 1997; later releases are not included in that statement.
  • Sugarland: 22 million album sales worldwide reported in Kristian Bush’s bio.
  • Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers: 80 million album sales worldwide reported in 2014; source does not separate Petty’s solo catalog.
